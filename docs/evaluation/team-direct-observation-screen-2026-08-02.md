# Direct team observation screen — 2026-08-02

This is a zero-cost, development-overlap screen of a tempting world-knowledge
increment: expose a high-confidence printed team observation when the canonical
team field is empty. It uses the 150-card `high-150` exhaustive observation
replay, excludes Lot grammar, and renders with the current production Composer
except that eBay's `search_optimization` suppression is disabled for the
candidate arm.

## Result

| Cards with a candidate team | Wins | Losses | Ties | Δ macro F1 | Reference-loss cards | Over 80 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 98 | 12 | 83 | 3 | -0.066053 | 24 | 0 |

The candidate added the team after the normal title fields. Even when a team
is visibly printed, it often consumes precision budget without appearing in
the reviewed marketplace title, or forces a higher-value product/finish token
out during compression. The observed team is therefore evidence, not title
authority.

## Decision

Do not restore `team` globally, and do not add a generic “printed team”
exception to CSM/SEM. The result is a clear negative asset. A future team
experiment would need an independently validated player-season/team registry
and a marketplace-specific inclusion policy; that is a new mechanism, not a
reason to weaken the current suppression.

This screen is evaluation-only and made no provider calls or production
changes.
